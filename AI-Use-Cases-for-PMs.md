
# AI Use-Cases for ALL PMs (Portfolio, Program, Project, and Product Managers)

*NOTE:* This document is under construction.

## Program and Project Scoping

- Generation of program/project objectives from raw notes and/or raw requirements
- Requirements intake from disparate stakeholders, requirements analysis, organization and prioritization, and generation of requirements documentation
- Generation of program/project scope documentation from proposals, contracts, legal requirements, charters, or other starting documentation

## Program and Project Planning

- Generation of Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) from high-level, raw notes
- Filling in gaps of incomplete task lists / breaking high-level program or project goals into discrete tasks
- Timeline estimation based on program/project team make-up, historical program/project data, estimated team capacity, known dependencies, and task lists
- Automated project structure and task creation in project management tools like JIRA, using requirements documentation, scope information, or raw notes

## Program and Project Execution

- Recommendations on efficiency improvements based on team retrospective feedback analysis, complemented by additional inputs such as project data, anecdotal notes, or other inputs
- Resource allocation for programs or projects based on available skills, task lists, known dependencies, budget constraints, or other factors; ongoing schedule or allocation adjustments based on new information
- Predictive risk identification and management based on program or project data, known constraints, new dependencies, or other factors
- Automated knowledge management, including generation of meeting notes and next steps, project design documentation, decision and risk logs, and other needs
- Workflow automation for program/project teams that span engineering, product, commercial, customer support, security, or other teams; passing critical information quickly onto relevant stakeholders from project management tools, application actions and/or activity logs, status reports, meeting notes, etc.

## Program and Project Monitoring

- Creation of customer feedback pipelines, enabling feedback to be ingested, analyzed, organized, and prioritized more quickly
- Recommendation of improvements and/or next steps based on customer feedback, new market analysis, or other factors
- Automated status reporting and dashboard generation using program/project data, team efficiency data, and other inputs
